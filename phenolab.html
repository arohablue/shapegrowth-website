Step 01

Capture

Place your plant in the PHENOLAB scanner. Our multi-modal imaging array captures high-resolution RGB, depth, and hyperspectral data simultaneously — in under 60 seconds per plant.

Step 02

Analyze

Our AI pipeline processes the 3D point cloud to extract hundreds of morphological traits — leaf area, stem diameter, branch architecture, canopy volume — with sub-millimeter precision.

Step 03

Act

Access your results through a clean research dashboard or programmatically via REST API. Compare genotypes, track growth curves, and export structured datasets for downstream statistical analysis.
